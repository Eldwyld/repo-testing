Typography

Cinzel for every heading, Inter for everything else, Cormorant for editorial asides. The contrast between the first two is the typographic identity.

body · Inter

Lede — 1.25rem, line-height 1.625, gray-600, capped at 48rem.

Default body copy sits at 14px with a 1.55 line-height. The Inter feature settings cv02 cv03 cv04 cv11 switch on the single-storey a, the open g and the tailed l — they soften the face noticeably, and they are deliberate.
